How to Calculate Percentages

Calculating a percentage means finding how much one number is out of 100.
The basic percentage formula is:

Percentage (%) = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

Example 1 – Find What Percent One Number Is of Another

Question: What percent is 25 of 200?
Solution: (25 ÷ 200) × 100 = 12.5%

Example 2 – Find a Percentage of a Number

Question: What is 20% of 150?
Solution: (20 ÷ 100) × 150 = 30

Example 3 – Percentage Increase

Formula: ((New – Old) ÷ Old) × 100
Example: From 500 to 600 → ((600 – 500) ÷ 500) × 100 = 20% increase

Example 4 – Percentage Decrease

Formula: ((Old – New) ÷ Old) × 100
Example: From 20000 to 18000 → ((20000 – 18000) ÷ 20000) × 100 = 10% decrease

Quick Tip

To convert a decimal to a percentage, multiply by 100.
Example: 0.75 × 100 = 75%

How do I calculate percentage increase or decrease?

Use these simple formulas:

  • Percentage Increase: ((New – Old) ÷ Old) × 100

  • Percentage Decrease: ((Old – New) ÷ Old) × 100
    Example: If a price rises from 500 to 600, the percentage increase is 20%.
    These are common in salary hikes, sales growth, and business profit calculations.

How can I find discount percentage while shopping?

To find the discount percentage, use:
(Original Price – Sale Price) ÷ Original Price × 100
Example: ₹2000 reduced to ₹1600 → (2000 – 1600) ÷ 2000 × 100 = 20% discount.
This helps you quickly calculate sale prices and offers using our discount percentage calculator.
